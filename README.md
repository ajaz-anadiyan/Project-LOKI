## 🌌 What is FSO?

**Free-Space Optical Communication (FSO)** uses **laser beams 🔴** to send information through open space.

Instead of using only traditional **radio waves**, NASA can use highly focused beams of light to send huge amounts of data between spacecraft and Earth.

```text
🛰️ SPACECRAFT
      │
      │ 🔴 LASER DATA
      ▼
    🌌 SPACE
      │
      ▼
🔭 EARTH RECEIVER
```

| -------------------- | ------------------------ |

## 🛰️ NASA Projects

### 🔵 LCRD

**Laser Communications Relay Demonstration** — tested how laser communication can relay information through space.

### 🟣 TBIRD

Demonstrated extremely high-speed optical data transmission from a small satellite.

### 🔴 DSOC

**Deep Space Optical Communications** — tested laser communication across deep space using NASA's **Psyche spacecraft**.


##  The Challenge

Laser beams are incredibly narrow.

That means NASA has to point them **extremely accurately** 🎯.

Plus, clouds and Earth's atmosphere ☁️ can interfere with signals reaching ground stations.

##  The Future

FSO could help connect:

# 🌍 → 🌕 → 🔴 → 🌌

As space missions collect more data, laser communication could become a major part of the future of space exploration.
